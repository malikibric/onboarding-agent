
# Altruist Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

Altruist is a modern, digital-first custodian and investment management platform built exclusively for independent Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs).

It combines self-clearing brokerage and custody with integrated software for account opening, trading, automated rebalancing, reporting, billing, and a client portal—all in one system.

Altruist was founded in 2018, has offices in Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Dallas, and has grown to serve thousands of advisors, becoming one of the largest custodians by advisors served.


## Mission and Product Impact

Altruist’s mission is to make independent financial advice better, more affordable, and more accessible to everyone.

This mission translates into:

- Lower fees for RIAs.
- Streamlined digital workflows instead of paper-heavy processes.
- Tools that give advisors more time to do real financial planning with clients.


## Who Uses Altruist and Why

Altruist’s primary users are independent RIAs and wealth management firms that want integrated custody, trading, and portfolio management software rather than a patchwork of legacy tools.

Typical reasons firms choose Altruist include:

- Faster digital account opening.
- Fractional share trading.
- Automated portfolio rebalancing.
- Better reporting.
- A modern client experience that compares favorably to Schwab- and Fidelity-style platforms.


## Core Platform Capabilities: Advisor View

From an advisor’s perspective, Altruist provides:

- **Digital account opening:** Workflows that reduce onboarding from weeks to minutes.
- **Trading:** Commission-free fractional trading in stocks and ETFs, plus support for mutual funds and model portfolios.
- **Portfolio management:** Automated, rules-based portfolio rebalancing through a unified advisor dashboard.
- **Reporting and billing:** Performance reporting and billing tools accessible from the same platform.

The onboarding agent should explain each concept at a high level and map it to real advisor tasks, such as:

- Opening a household.
- Applying a model.
- Running billing.


## Client Experience: End-Investor View

Altruist offers a web and mobile experience where investors can:

- View their accounts.
- Track performance.
- Move cash.
- Access statements with a few clicks.

Clients typically receive an invitation from their advisor, create a login, and use the portal to monitor progress toward goals and stay informed about their investments.

New hires should understand that advisors drive strategy and relationships, while Altruist powers the digital experience clients see day to day.


## Portfolio Management, Tax Features, and Alternatives

Altruist’s portfolio management tools support:

- Manual trading.
- Automated rebalancing.
- Custom models and unified managed accounts (UMAs).
- Personalized indexing, including direct indexing with screens and exclusions.

Built-in tax management includes:

- Daily scanning for tax-loss harvesting.
- Tax-sensitive rebalancing.
- Features that help advisors deliver tax-efficient portfolios at scale.

The platform has also expanded to include access to alternative assets, margin lending, options, and faster money movement. This allows advisors to offer more sophisticated strategies without leaving the core system.


## Integrations and Ecosystem

Altruist integrates with common RIA tools, including:

- CRMs.
- Financial planning platforms.
- Portfolio accounting systems.

These integrations allow firms to bring existing workflows into a more modern custodial environment.

Hazel AI also connects to CRM, email, calendar, and document systems, helping unify firm knowledge and reduce manual administrative work.


## Hazel AI: Overview for New Hires

Hazel is an AI platform built by Altruist specifically for wealth management. It originally emerged from the acquisition of Thyme, a meeting-intelligence tool.

Hazel automates:

- Meeting preparation.
- Note-taking.
- Follow-ups.
- CRM updates.
- Document analysis.
- Complex “what-if” scenarios.

It acts like an AI engine that understands a firm’s clients, communications, and data.

Hazel is available both to firms that custody with Altruist and to other firms. When paired with Altruist, Hazel can use live account, holdings, and balance data to provide highly personalized insights.


### Hazel Capabilities Relevant to Onboarding

For a new hire, Hazel’s capabilities can be summarized as follows:

- **Ask Hazel:** Natural-language Q&A over client emails, meetings, CRM notes, and documents.
- **Meeting intelligence:** Record, transcribe, and summarize meetings; surface action items; and push them to CRM or task systems.
- **Daily digest:** A prioritized view of upcoming meetings, important emails, and follow-ups based on firm activity.
- **Tax planning:** Ingest tax returns, paystubs, statements, and custodian data to propose personalized tax strategies and explain them in plain language.

The onboarding agent should help new hires see Hazel as a co-pilot for their day rather than a generic chatbot. Emphasize how it connects to the firm’s actual data and workflows.


## Security, Compliance, and Data Handling

Altruist operates as a FINRA-registered broker-dealer and custodian with SIPC protection. It is subject to SEC and FINRA rules and independent audits of its controls.

Client assets are held according to standard industry protections. Altruist emphasizes secure handling of financial data and strong operational controls.

Hazel’s security messaging emphasizes that:

- Client data is not used to train foundation models.
- Data is encrypted.
- Enterprise-grade security is central to its design.
- Zero-data-retention arrangements with AI providers are used where applicable.


## FAQ for the Onboarding Agent

### What is Altruist?

> Altruist is a modern custodian and investment management platform built for independent RIAs. It combines custody, trading, portfolio management, reporting, billing, and a client portal into one integrated system, with a focus on digital workflows and lower fees.

### Who typically uses Altruist?

> Independent financial advisors and RIA firms use Altruist to open and manage client accounts, trade and rebalance portfolios, run billing and reporting, and deliver a modern client experience without relying on multiple legacy systems.

### What is Hazel, and how does it relate to Altruist?

> Hazel is an AI platform built for wealth management. It connects to CRM, email, calendar, and documents to automate meeting preparation, notes, follow-ups, and tax planning. When a firm also uses Altruist, Hazel can pull real-time custodial data—including accounts, holdings, and balances—to provide more precise, personalized insights.

### Do I need to understand all the technical details to start?

> No. As a new hire, it’s most important to understand Altruist’s role as the firm’s custodian and operating system, the basic advisor and client workflows, and how Hazel helps you prepare for meetings, follow up with clients, and manage tasks. You can learn deeper features over time.

### How does Altruist think about security and compliance?

> Altruist is a regulated custodian and broker-dealer with FINRA/SIPC membership and SEC/FINRA oversight. It uses modern security practices, and Hazel is designed so that client data is encrypted and not used to train general AI models.
