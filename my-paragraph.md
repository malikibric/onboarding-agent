I recently started using AI-assisted codebase auditing and evaluation workflows to review agent behavior more systematically and improve the quality of my development process. It has been particularly useful for tracing failures back to their underlying causes, checking whether prompts, policies, knowledge sources, and tests are aligned, and identifying gaps that ordinary test suites might overlook. 
I also use AI to compare the system’s documented safety goals with its actual implementation, especially around refusal behavior, source attribution, uncertainty, and unsupported claims. Rather than treating its output as automatically correct, I use it as a review and reasoning tool, verify its findings against the code and test results, and make the final decisions myself. This approach has helped me work more carefully, communicate technical issues more clearly, and make safer, more evidence-based improvements to the project.
