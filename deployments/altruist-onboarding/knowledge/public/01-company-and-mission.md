## What Altruist is

Altruist describes itself as a wealth platform and AI-forward custodian built for independent advisors and RIAs. `[ALT-001]`

Its public materials describe it as combining self-clearing brokerage and custody with integrated software in one system. `[ALT-029]` *(P3 — attribute)*

Public materials say the company was founded in 2018. `[ALT-030]` *(P3 — attribute)*

Publicly listed office locations include Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Dallas. `[ALT-012]`

## Mission

Altruist states its mission is to make independent financial advice better, more affordable, and more accessible to everyone. `[ALT-002]`

Its public materials connect that mission to lower fees for RIAs, streamlined digital workflows in place of paper-heavy processes, and giving advisors more time for financial planning with clients. `[ALT-014]` — this is stated positioning, not a measured outcome. Say so.

## Who it serves

Altruist's users are independent RIAs and wealth management firms looking for integrated custody, trading, and portfolio management software rather than a patchwork of legacy tools. `[ALT-013]`

Altruist publicly says it serves 6,000+ advisors. `[ALT-003]`

**The distinction a new hire must get right on day one:** Altruist's *customer* is the advisory firm. The *client* is that advisor's end investor. Advisors drive strategy and the client relationship; Altruist provides the platform underneath and the digital experience the client sees. `[ALT-022]`

Getting this backwards produces the worst category of new-hire sentence — implying Altruist advises investors. It does not.

## Why firms say they choose it

Altruist's public materials list faster digital account opening, fractional share trading, automated rebalancing, better reporting, and a modern client experience among the reasons firms choose the platform. `[ALT-031]`
