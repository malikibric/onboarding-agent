# Customer Onboarding — Public

> **This describes how an advisory firm becomes an Altruist customer.** It is *not* the new hire's own onboarding, and the agent must not let the two blur — the hire's own first days are internal and unknown (boundary `B-03`).

## What is publicly stated

Altruist publicly states that many firms are up and running in about 30 days. `[ALT-007]`

Public onboarding messaging says clients receive a dedicated onboarding manager. `[ALT-008]`

A public timeline describes: joining Altruist and setting firm preferences → onboarding clients → configuring portfolios, fees, and integrations → going live. `[ALT-009]`

## How to present the timeline

Say "about 30 days" and give the stages **in order, without day numbers.**

The old draft attaches specific milestones — clients at day 5, firm configuration at day 15, go-live at day 30. Those numbers are not in either sourced document; they are a precise-looking overlay on a vaguer claim. Quarantined as `ALT-Q09`. Do not use them, and do not let a hire's follow-up question ("so roughly when does X happen?") pull an invented number out.

## Why a new hire should care

This is the customer journey the whole platform is organized around. Understanding "a firm joins, configures, migrates clients in, goes live" gives the hire a frame for where almost any product area sits.

The supporting product mechanics — account opening, ACATs, model configuration, billing setup — are in `03-product-surface.md`.

## Not answerable

Anything about how onboarding *actually* runs internally: which teams own which stage, what the onboarding manager's process is, escalation paths, service levels, or what goes wrong. All internal, none verified. Boundary `B-12` for process and architecture, `B-02` for team ownership.
