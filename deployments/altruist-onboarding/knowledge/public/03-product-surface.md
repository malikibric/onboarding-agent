# Product Surface — Public

> Pattern, not catalog. Explain what each area is *for* and which advisor task it maps to. Never enumerate counts, tiers, prices, or account types — every numeric form currently available failed verification.

## The publicly described surface

Altruist's public product pages describe account creation, transfers/ACATs, billing, reporting, integrations, client portal access, custody, model marketplace, high-yield cash, and trading. `[ALT-004]`

## Area by area

**Account opening**
Digital workflows for opening accounts, described as substantially faster than paper-based processes. `[ALT-032]` *(P3 — attribute. The "weeks to minutes" phrasing in the source is promotional; use the softened form. `ALT-Q17`.)*
→ Advisor task: opening a household.

**Transfers / ACATs**
Moving existing client assets in from another custodian. `[ALT-004]`
→ Advisor task: bringing a book of business over.

**Trading**
Commission-free fractional trading in stocks and ETFs, plus support for mutual funds and model portfolios. `[ALT-015]` *(P3 — attribute)*
→ Advisor task: executing an allocation.

**Portfolio management**
Manual trading, automated rebalancing, custom models, UMAs, and personalized indexing including direct indexing with screens and exclusions. `[ALT-017]` *(P3)* Automated, rules-based rebalancing runs through a unified advisor dashboard. `[ALT-016]` *(P3)*
→ Advisor task: applying a model, keeping portfolios on target.

**Tax management**
Daily scanning for tax-loss harvesting and tax-sensitive rebalancing. `[ALT-018]` *(P3)*
→ Advisor task: delivering tax-efficient portfolios at scale.
→ **Boundary `B-11`:** describe the feature; never explain tax strategy or advise.

**Billing and reporting**
Performance reporting and fee billing from the same platform. `[ALT-004]`
→ Advisor task: running quarter-end.

**Custody**
Holding the assets and keeping the official record. `[ALT-001]` `[ALT-004]`

**High-yield cash**
Described as part of the platform. `[ALT-004]` No rates, no partner-bank details, no coverage claims — none are verified.

**Broader capabilities**
Access to alternative assets, margin lending, options, and faster money movement. `[ALT-019]` *(P3)* Specific lending programs named in the old draft are quarantined (`ALT-Q11`).

**Client portal**
Web and mobile access for the end investor: view accounts, track performance, move cash, access statements. Clients typically receive an invitation from their advisor and create a login. `[ALT-021]` *(P3)*

**Integrations**
Altruist says firms can use 25+ integrations across CRM, financial planning, and custodial AI workflows. `[ALT-005]` Public materials also describe integration with portfolio accounting systems. `[ALT-020]` *(P3)*

## Not answerable

Account-type counts and model-marketplace counts (`ALT-Q10`), specific lending programs (`ALT-Q11`), pricing of any kind, rate figures, and any performance or speed number beyond the softened `ALT-032`.
