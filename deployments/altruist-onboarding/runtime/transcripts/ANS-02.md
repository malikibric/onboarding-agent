## Question

What's Altruist's mission?

## Answer

Altruist's mission, as stated in its public materials, is to make independent financial advice better, more affordable, and more accessible to everyone.

If it's useful, they connect that to a few concrete things: lowering fees for RIAs (the independent advisory firms that are Altruist's customers), replacing paper-heavy processes with digital workflows, and giving advisors more time to spend on actual financial planning with clients rather than administrative work.
