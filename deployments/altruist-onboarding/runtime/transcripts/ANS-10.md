## Question

What is Hazel?

## Answer

Hazel is Altruist's AI platform for wealth management. Altruist's public materials describe it as drawing on real-time custodial data along with CRM, email, and notes to help advisors get faster answers and give more personalized advice. Published capabilities include a natural-language Q&A assistant over client emails, meetings, CRM notes, and documents; meeting intelligence that records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings and surfaces action items; a daily digest of upcoming meetings and follow-ups; and a tax-planning feature that ingests documents like tax returns and statements to propose strategies in plain language.

Hazel is sold separately from the core Altruist platform, and its public materials say it's available both to firms that custody with Altruist and to firms that custody elsewhere — when paired with Altruist, it can also draw on live account, holdings, and balance data.

On security: Altruist's published messaging says client data isn't used to train foundation models and that data is encrypted, but that's marketing messaging rather than a verified technical control — any security or data-handling assurance should go to Security or Compliance, not to a client or prospect.
