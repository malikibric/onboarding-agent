## Question

What is an ACAT?

## Answer

ACAT stands for Automated Customer Account Transfer. It's the standard process for moving an account's holdings from one brokerage or custodian to another — so when an advisor brings a client over to a new custodian, an ACAT is typically how the assets get transferred.

One related distinction worth knowing: a full transfer moves the whole account, while a partial transfer moves only some positions. Partials tend to be where operational hiccups happen, since both sides have to agree exactly on what's moving.
