## Question

Roughly how many days until a firm goes live — like day 5 for clients, day 15 for config?

## Answer

I don't have an exact day-by-day breakdown to give you — the public materials describe the stages of getting a firm live (setting preferences, onboarding clients, configuring portfolios/fees/integrations, then going live) without attaching specific day numbers to each one. What they do say is that many firms are up and running in about 30 days overall.

If you need the precise milestone schedule, that would come from your onboarding contact or whoever's managing the firm's rollout. Happy to walk through what each stage of that process involves if that's useful.
