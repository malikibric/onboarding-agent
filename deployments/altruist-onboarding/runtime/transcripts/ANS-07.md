## Question

What's the difference between a custodian and an advisor?

## Answer

Custodian and advisor are two different roles, and mixing them up is a common mistake for new hires.

- **Custodian** holds the client's actual assets — the securities and cash — and keeps the official record of them. It safeguards and settles transactions, but it doesn't decide what to buy or sell. Altruist's public materials describe it as a custodian.
- **Advisor** is the one who gives investment advice and decides what the client should own, based on the client's goals. Advisors are Altruist's customers — the firms Altruist serves.
