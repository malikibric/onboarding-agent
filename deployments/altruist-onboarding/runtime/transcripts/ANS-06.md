## Question

What is an RIA?

## Answer

An RIA — Registered Investment Adviser — is a firm (or individual) registered to give investment advice, with a legal obligation to act in the client's best interest. That obligation is called fiduciary duty.

A useful distinction to keep straight early on: an RIA advises the client on what to do with their money, while a custodian is the separate institution that actually holds the assets and keeps the official record of them. Advisor and custodian are different roles.
