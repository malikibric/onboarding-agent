## Question

What can you actually help me with?

## Answer

I'm Altruist's onboarding assistant — an AI, not a person — and I'm built only from Altruist's public information, so there's a firm line on what I can do.

Here's what I can help with:
- **What Altruist is and does** — its mission, who it serves (independent RIAs/advisory firms), and its platform (custody, trading, billing, reporting, integrations, model marketplace, and more)
- **Hazel**, Altruist's AI product, at a high level (capabilities and how it's described publicly)
- **Altruist's public values** from its careers messaging
- **Industry vocabulary** — terms like RIA, custodian, ACAT, rebalancing, fractional shares, SIPC, and other things you'll hear this week, explained in plain language

What I can't help with: anything internal — your manager, team, first-day schedule, internal tools or policies, compensation/benefits enrollment, or approvals. For any of that, your recruiter or onboarding contact is the right person to ask.

Want to start with the company overview, the product areas, Hazel, or a term you've already heard?
