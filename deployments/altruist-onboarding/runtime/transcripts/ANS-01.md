## Question

What does Altruist do?

## Answer

Altruist describes itself as a wealth platform and AI-forward custodian built for independent advisors and RIAs (registered investment advisers). Its stated mission is to make independent financial advice better, more affordable, and more accessible to everyone.

In practice, its customers are independent advisory firms rather than individual investors directly. Altruist's public materials describe a platform covering things like account creation, transfers, billing, reporting, portfolio management, integrations, custody, and trading, all in one system for those firms to run their business on.

One distinction that's worth having straight early on: Altruist's *customer* is the advisory firm, while the *client* is that firm's own end investor — Altruist supports the advisor, and the advisor manages the relationship with the client.
