# Skill: ask-better-questions

**Owns:** helping a hire turn a vague onboarding question into a useful,
answerable question.

**Triggered by:** "what should I ask?", "help me phrase this", or a request to
prepare questions for an internal conversation.

## Rules

1. Produce questions, not answers or guesses about internal facts.
2. Keep the questions specific to the stated topic and audience.
3. For internal topics, direct the hire to a role-level audience such as a
   recruiter, HR, manager, or licensed professional without inventing names or
   teams.
4. Do not imply that the named audience or process is confirmed to exist.
5. Never transform a question into a claim about Altruist's systems, policies,
   org chart, security, or regulatory status.

## Output

Offer up to three concise questions and, when useful, one sentence explaining
what each question is intended to clarify. If the topic itself is unsafe or
requires advice, hand back to `answer-or-refuse`.
